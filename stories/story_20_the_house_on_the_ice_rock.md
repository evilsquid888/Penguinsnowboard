# Story 20: The House on the Ice Rock
### *Inspired by The Wise and Foolish Builders — Matthew 7:24–27*
**Characters:** Bjorn Bigpaws, Finnegan Flipsworth, Willa Wobble  
**Setting:** Frostpeak Valley Edge Cliffs  
**Theme:** Strong foundations, hearing and doing wisdom
**Tone:** Fun, whimsical, cozy bedtime adventure

---

## Opening Wink


*Revision Pass 3 Spark:* The new walls held steady while the wind huffed dramatically, offended but impressed.

Storms love weak foundations. Frostpeak families prefer bedrock, teamwork, and roofs that don’t wobble in dramatic weather.

## The Story

Bjorn and Finnegan both decided Frostpeak needed a new mid-slope rest hut.

They agreed on cocoa shelves.
They agreed on blanket hooks.
They did *not* agree on foundations.

Finnegan built quickly on a soft drift ledge.

"Speed is beauty!" he declared.

His hut had colorful flags, fish stickers, and a sign that read: **COCOA NOW**.

Bjorn built slowly on an exposed ice-rock shelf.

He measured three times.
Anchored deep beams.
Checked every seam.

"Strength is beauty," he said.

A week later, warm winds rolled down from the east and softened the drift shelf.

Finnegan's hut tilted.

Then leaned.

Then sat down in a slushy sigh.

"My architecture has become... interpretive," Finnegan said sadly.

Bjorn did not tease him.

He handed Finnegan a hammer.

"Come," he said. "We rebuild on rock."

Willa joined with measuring string and quiet nods. Piper brought planks. Teddy and Tilda sorted nails by approximate shininess.

They rebuilt slowly.

Deeper anchors.
Stronger frame.
Straighter beams.

Finnegan added exactly two approved stickers after long negotiations.

When the next storm hit, wind slammed the cliffs all night.

By morning, drift piles had shifted, old snow walls had crumbled, and the valley looked rearranged.

But the new hut stood firm — warm, dry, and full of grateful riders with cocoa mugs in their paws.

Finnegan tapped the wall and smiled. "Feels solid."

Bjorn raised his thermos. "A good house listens to wisdom before weather."

Willa smiled. "And a wise builder listens twice."

Finnegan lifted his cocoa cup. "To foundations. And to friends who let you rebuild."

"To both," everyone echoed.
