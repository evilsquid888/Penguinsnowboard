# Story 20: The House on the Ice Rock
### *Inspired by The Wise and Foolish Builders — Matthew 7:24–27*
**Characters:** Bjorn Bigpaws, Finnegan Flipsworth, Willa Wobble  
**Setting:** Frostpeak Valley edge cliffs  
**Theme:** Strong foundations, hearing and doing wisdom

---

## The Story

Bjorn and Finnegan built two tiny rest huts for tired riders.

Finnegan built fast on soft drift snow: bright flags, fish stickers, instant cocoa shelf.

Bjorn built slowly on a rocky ice ledge: deep anchors, thick beams, patient lines.

A week later, warm winds came.

Drift snow melted.

Finnegan's hut tilted, sighed, and sat down in a puddle.

"My architecture has become abstract," he said sadly.

Bjorn said nothing — just handed him a hammer.

Together they rebuilt on the rock. Willa measured. Finnegan learned to anchor posts. Bjorn allowed exactly two stickers.

When the next storm hit, the hut stood firm — lights glowing, cocoa steaming, everyone safe inside.

Finnegan raised his mug. "To foundations. And to friends who let you rebuild."

"To both," Bjorn said.
