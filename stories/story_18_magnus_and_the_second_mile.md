# Story 18: Magnus and the Second Mile
### *Inspired by Go the Second Mile — Matthew 5:41*
**Characters:** Magnus Meltsworth, Piper Paddlefoot, Bjorn Bigpaws  
**Setting:** Long Traverse Trail  
**Theme:** Extra kindness, strength in service

---

## The Story

A visiting fox twisted her ankle halfway up Long Traverse Trail.

Magnus carried her pack to the first lodge.

At the lodge, she whispered, embarrassed, "Could someone carry it to the far cabins too?"

Magnus looked tired. Then he lifted the pack again.

"Fine," he said. "One more mile."

Piper joined with blankets. Bjorn brought tea. Together they made a slow caravan through moonlit snow.

At the cabins, the fox cried with relief. "You already did enough."

Magnus shrugged. "Sometimes enough has a second half."

Back home, he insisted his shoulders were fine.

Nobody believed him, but everyone admired him.

## Goodnight Blessing

*The extra mile is where love shines bright.*
