# Story 14: Piper on the Moonlit Ice
### *Inspired by Peter Walks on Water — Matthew 14:22–33*
**Characters:** Piper Paddlefoot, Aurora Frostholm, Bjorn Bigpaws  
**Setting:** Frozen Mirror Lake  
**Theme:** Courage to step out, trust when wobbling
**Tone:** Fun, whimsical, cozy bedtime adventure

---

> *Some brave moments are loud. Some are quiet and shaky. Both still count.*

## Opening Wink

Moonlit ice is beautiful, wobbly, and deeply committed to testing everyone’s balance and bravery.

## The Story

On a clear silver night, Frozen Mirror Lake stretched wide and dark, like a giant polished gem under the moon.

Aurora Frostholm stood at the shoreline, skis sparkling in the light. "Let's cross the center path tonight," she said. "The part no one uses after sunset."

Piper swallowed. "The center looks... very big."

"Exactly," Aurora said with a grin.

Bjorn Bigpaws stood by the shore with a coiled safety rope and his thermos. "I will remain here, dignified and unconcerned," he announced.

Piper narrowed her eyes. "You're concerned."

Bjorn sniffed. "Profoundly concerned."

Piper stepped onto the open ice.

One glide. Smooth.

Two glides. Clean.

Three glides. Wonderful.

Then she looked down.

Beneath the translucent ice, old frozen cracks ran like silver lightning. Deep. Endless. Her chest tightened.

The more she stared, the more she felt herself wobbling.

A wind gust pushed her board sideways. She dropped to one knee.

"AURORA!" she shouted.

Aurora turned instantly and reached out one paw. "Eyes up, Piper. Not down. Come to me one breath at a time."

"I can't," Piper whispered.

"You can," Aurora said calmly. "I'm right here."

Piper took a breath.

She stood.

Another breath.

One tiny glide.

Another breath.

One more.

A hard gust hit and she slipped again, board skittering.

"Piper!" Bjorn called from shore, rope ready.

Piper squeezed her eyes shut for half a second, then opened them and fixed on Aurora's face.

Not the ice.
Not the cracks.
Not the fear.

Just Aurora.

She pushed once more and reached Aurora's paw.

Aurora gripped tight. "There you are," she said.

Together they crossed the final stretch to the far bank, laughing breathlessly when their boards touched snow.

On the way back, they moved in tandem, turning together and keeping their eyes on each other whenever wind rose.

At shore, Bjorn wrapped Piper in an enormous blanket and poured warm tea into a tiny cup.

Piper held it with trembling flippers.

"I thought courage meant never wobbling," she said.

Aurora sat beside her. "No. Courage means wobbling and moving anyway."

Bjorn nodded. "Courage is often just the next small step taken before panic finishes speaking."

Piper looked back over the moonlit lake.

It still looked big.

But now big did not mean impossible.

She smiled into her tea.

"Tomorrow night," she said, "same path."

Aurora grinned. "I'll race you."

"Last one buys cocoa," Bjorn added.

And all three agreed that fear had lost this round.
