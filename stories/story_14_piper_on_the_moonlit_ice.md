# Story 14: Piper on the Moonlit Ice
### *Inspired by Peter Walks on Water — Matthew 14:22–33*
**Characters:** Piper Paddlefoot, Aurora Frostholm, Bjorn Bigpaws  
**Setting:** Frozen Mirror Lake  
**Theme:** Courage to step out, trust when wobbling

---

## The Story

On a clear moonlit night, Aurora pointed toward the dark center of Frozen Mirror Lake.

"Let's cross where nobody crosses," she said.

Piper's belly flipped. "That part looks... very big."

"Exactly," Aurora grinned.

Bjorn stayed near shore with a safety line. "Call if needed," he said, pretending not to worry.

Piper stepped onto the open ice. For three glides, she felt fearless.

Then she looked down.

Deep frozen cracks beneath her board looked like silver lightning. Her knees shook.

"AURORA!"

Aurora turned and reached out. "Eyes up, Piper. Not down. Come one breath at a time."

Piper breathed. Pushed. Wobbled. Breathed again.

A gust hit. She dropped to one knee.

"I can't!"

"You can," Aurora said. "I'm right here."

Piper took one more tiny glide and grabbed Aurora's paw.

Together they crossed the final stretch and reached the far bank laughing with relief.

Back at shore, Bjorn wrapped Piper in a blanket.

"A bear of my stature was not worried," he said.

"You were," Piper smiled.

"Profoundly," Bjorn admitted.

Piper looked at the lake. "I thought courage meant never wobbling."

Aurora shook her head. "Courage means wobbling and continuing."
