# Story 11: The Night the Wind Stopped
### *Inspired by Jesus Calms the Storm — Mark 4:35–41*
**Characters:** Piper Paddlefoot, Finnegan Flipsworth, Nora Snowmane, Magnus Meltsworth  
**Setting:** Frozen Mirror Lake, Starwatch Cliff  
**Theme:** Peace in fear, trust in the middle of the storm
**Tone:** Fun, whimsical, cozy bedtime adventure

---

## The Story

On the calmest nights in Frostpeak Valley, the surface of Frozen Mirror Lake looked like polished silver.

On this night, it looked like trouble.

Piper and Finnegan had talked Magnus into one "tiny" moonlight ride on the lake — just a quick glide from one shore to the other under the northern lights.

"It'll be peaceful," Piper said.

"It'll be educational," Finnegan said.

"It'll be short," both said.

Magnus squinted at both of them. "When you two say short, it means long. When you two say peaceful, it means loud."

Still, he came.

They pushed off, skimming over wind-dusted snow on top of the ice. For a while, it was perfect. Stars overhead. Frosty breath in little clouds. Their boards and skis whispering over the surface.

Then the wind changed.

It came down from the ridge in a hard, sudden burst — sharp and wild. Snow spiraled across the lake. The lights in the sky vanished behind thick cloud. The quiet became a howl.

Finnegan yelled, "WOOOHOOOO—" then swallowed a mouthful of snow. "I MEAN AAAAAAH!"

Piper crouched lower on her board. She could barely see the tip of it. Magnus planted his poles, trying to steady all three of them.

The wind shoved them sideways.

The ice groaned underneath.

Piper's heart began to race. She hated how fast fear made everything feel small and loud at the same time.

"We're going to crash into the east rocks!" she shouted.

"No," Magnus barked. "We're not. Stay low. Stay near me."

Another blast hit and spun Finnegan in a full accidental circle. "I AM A BEAUTIFUL DISASTER!"

"Not helping!" Piper shouted.

Then, through the flying snow, a steady shape appeared.

A white figure. Strong, calm, unhurried.

Nora Snowmane.

She moved toward them like the storm had simply forgotten to trouble her. Rope looped over one shoulder. Lantern in one paw. Eyes warm and certain.

"Nora!" Piper cried.

"There you are," said Nora, as if meeting them at cocoa hour. "I thought you'd be out here."

"The wind is insane!" Finnegan shouted.

Nora nodded once. "Yes. So let's not be." She set the lantern between them, low and shielded. "Listen to me."

Her voice was not loud, but it cut through everything.

"One breath in," she said. "One breath out."

Piper did it.

Finnegan did it.

Magnus did it, pretending he was not doing it.

"Again," Nora said.

The wind still roared. The lake still shook. But inside the circle of lantern-light, their breathing slowed.

"Storms are loud," Nora said. "That doesn't mean they are in charge."

She tied the rope around Magnus's waist, then around Piper, then around Finnegan.

"Now," she said, "we move together. No hero moves. No panic moves. Just one steady move at a time."

They began crossing toward the safer west bank, leaning into the wind as one line.

When Finnegan slipped, Magnus caught him.

When Piper wobbled, Nora steadied her shoulder.

When Magnus lost footing on a drift, Piper anchored the rope and shouted, "Got you!"

They moved slowly, but they moved.

At last, their boards scraped snow on the shore.

They collapsed in a heap beside a low ridge, panting and shivering and very, very relieved.

Nora planted the lantern in the snow and looked at all three.

"Well done," she said.

"I was terrified," Piper whispered.

"So was I," Finnegan said. "I was brave for exactly three seconds and then mostly screaming."

Magnus stared out at the lake. "I didn't like hearing the ice groan."

Nora sat beside them.

"Courage is not the absence of fear," she said. "Courage is choosing peace *while* fear is shouting."

Piper looked at the storm still whipping across the lake and then at the warm lantern light beside her.

"It still feels big," she said.

"Some things are big," Nora said kindly. "But fear does not get the final word."

They waited together until the clouds thinned and stars returned, one by one.

The wind softened.

The lake grew quiet again.

Finnegan looked up and sighed. "Okay. Next time we do moonlight rides, we bring Nora first."

"Next time," said Magnus, "we do cocoa. Indoors."

"Both," Nora said, smiling.

And they all agreed that both was excellent.

## Goodnight Blessing

*If your heart feels windy tonight,*  
*and your thoughts feel loud,*  
*may peace sit beside you like a lantern in snow.*  
*May your breathing slow.*  
*May your fear grow smaller than love.*  
*You are not alone on the ice.*  
*You are held.*  
*Goodnight, brave one.*
