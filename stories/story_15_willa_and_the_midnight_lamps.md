# Story 15: Willa and the Midnight Lamps
### *Inspired by The Wise and Foolish Virgins — Matthew 25:1–13*
**Characters:** Willa Wobble, Cleo Coldwater, Teddy & Tilda Powderpuff  
**Setting:** Starwatch Cliff Festival Trail  
**Theme:** Readiness, quiet wisdom
**Tone:** Fun, whimsical, cozy bedtime adventure

---

## Opening Wink

Smart riders pack what they need before dark. Willa calls this ‘wisdom.’ Finnegan calls it ‘unfair preparedness.’

## The Story

Every winter, Frostpeak Valley held the Midnight Lamps Parade.

At the first evening bell, everyone gathered at the trailhead with little snow-lanterns. The rule was simple: keep your lamp lit until you reached Starwatch Cliff.

Cleo packed sketch pencils.

Teddy packed marshmallows.

Tilda packed more marshmallows.

Willa packed extra candle stubs, spare wick, a striker, and one folded cloth to block wind.

"You're carrying half a workshop," Teddy said.

Willa smiled. "Night gets windy."

They climbed in happy chatter as stars thickened overhead.

Halfway up the ridge, the wind arrived fast and sharp. It cut around rocks and whipped lantern flames sideways.

One by one, lights winked out.

"Uh-oh," Teddy said.

"Big uh-oh," said Tilda.

Soon almost every lamp was dark except Willa's, which she sheltered with her cloth.

No one panicked.

Willa knelt in the snow and relit lamps one at a time, sharing extra candles carefully so each friend could keep a steady flame.

"Take one, pass one," she said.

Cleo held a lantern shield.

Teddy passed matches.

Tilda called out, "Next lamp patient!"

By the time they reached Starwatch Cliff, the whole line glowed again — dozens of tiny warm lights against the blue-white mountain night.

Then the northern lights unfurled overhead in green and violet ribbons.

Everyone gasped.

Cleo whispered, "How did you know to bring enough for everybody?"

Willa watched the sky and answered softly, "I didn't know exactly what would happen. I just planned for the part that usually does."

The twins looked at each other, solemn as judges.

With charcoal, they wrote on their mittens:

**PACK CANDLES.**

And under that:

**MAYBE LESS MARSHMALLOWS.**

They crossed that second line out immediately.

## Cinematic Prompt Enhancers (Phase 3)

Use these add-ons with every image/video prompt in this story to keep visuals consistent and lively.

- **Character Lock:** Keep **Willa + lamp team** visually consistent across shots (same outfit colors, board/ski design, and size relationships).
- **Style Lock:** cozy storybook fantasy, soft volumetric snowfall, aurora glow, warm rim-light, expressive faces, playful motion arcs.
- **Camera Mix:** include at least one **wide establishing shot**, one **medium character performance shot**, and one **low-angle action shot**.
- **Motion Cues:** snow spray, scarf flutter, tiny slips + recoveries, joyful group reactions.
- **Kid-Safe Tone:** wonder + comedy, never frightening; challenges feel adventurous, not scary.
- **Negative Prompt (optional):** no horror, no photoreal harshness, no gloomy desaturated palette, no weapons, no realistic peril.
