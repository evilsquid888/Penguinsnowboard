# Story 15: Willa and the Midnight Lamps
### *Inspired by The Wise and Foolish Virgins — Matthew 25:1–13*
**Characters:** Willa Wobble, Cleo Coldwater, Teddy & Tilda  
**Setting:** Starwatch Cliff festival trail  
**Theme:** Readiness, quiet wisdom

---

## The Story

Every winter, Frostpeak held the Midnight Lamps Parade. Everyone carried little snow-lanterns to Starwatch Cliff.

Willa packed extra candle nubs in her scarf pocket.

Teddy and Tilda packed marshmallows and exactly zero extra candles.

Halfway up, the wind sharpened. One by one, lamps went out.

"Uh-oh," Teddy whispered.

"Big uh-oh," Tilda agreed.

Willa shared her spare candles one at a time, keeping just enough for the final stretch.

By the time they reached the top, dozens of lamps glowed again around the cliff. The aurora rose green and violet over the valley.

"How did you know to bring extras?" Cleo asked.

Willa smiled. "I don't always know what will happen. I just know night gets windy."

The twins nodded very seriously and wrote on their mittens with charcoal: *PACK CANDLES.*
