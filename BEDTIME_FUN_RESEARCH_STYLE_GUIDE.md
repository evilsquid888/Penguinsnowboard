---

## What Great Bedtime Stories Tend to Share

1. **Comforting rhythm**
   - predictable section flow
   - repeated phrases/catchphrases
   - calm, reassuring endings

2. **Playful imagination**
   - absurd but safe situations
   - expressive, silly detail
   - gentle humor (never humiliating)

3. **Read-aloud clarity**
   - short-to-medium sentence musicality
   - clear emotional turns
   - voice-friendly lines for caregivers

4. **Warm attachment cues**
   - teamwork, belonging, rescue, welcome-home beats
   - bedtime blessing / settle-down close

5. **Thematic simplicity**
   - one main moral per story
   - concrete allegory elements
   - fun before lecture

---

## Frostpeak Fun Checklist (Use on Every Story Revision)

- [ ] Fun starts in first 150 words
- [ ] At least one laugh line every major scene
- [ ] Winter creature details are vivid (boards, scarves, snow physics)
- [ ] Allegory is visible but not preachy
- [ ] Stakes feel adventurous, never frightening
- [ ] Ending lands soft and cozy
- [ ] Goodnight Blessing supports sleep-down pace
- [ ] Image/Video prompts include action + mood + framing

---

## Frostpeak Voice Rules

- Keep narration warm, playful, and lightly mischievous
- Use kid-safe exaggeration (snow chaos, wipeouts, cocoa emergencies)
- Include recurring signature beats:
  - catchphrases
  - group reactions
  - winter sensory cues (snow hush, aurora glow, scarf flutter)
- Every challenge should have:
  - a funny beat
  - a heart beat
  - a hopeful beat

---

## Allegory Alignment Rules

For each story:
1. Name the biblical inspiration in plain language
2. Translate it into a winter challenge
3. Preserve the moral core
4. Add a playful creature/world detail
5. Close with comfort + belonging

---

## 3-Pass Revision Protocol

### Pass 1 — Fun Density
- Increase whimsy, visual specificity, and laugh moments
- tighten dry/expository lines

### Pass 2 — Allegory Clarity
- verify each story’s theme is clear, gentle, and coherent
- ensure winter metaphor supports the moral, not distracts from it

### Pass 3 — Bedtime Landing
- soften emotional transitions near the end
- keep ending restful, grateful, and safe

---

## Quality Gate (Ship only if all true)
- Story remains fun
- Story remains coherent
- Story remains bedtime-appropriate
- Story remains allegory-consistent
- Story remains winter-themed and silly
